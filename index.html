03 — Final Year Project

MedZone — Online
Pharmacy Platform.

MedZone is a full-stack online pharmacy platform built with the MERN stack as my Final Year Project. It connects patients, pharmacists, and admins in one seamless system with real-time features and AI integration.

🧑‍💊 Users can browse medicines and pharmacies, place orders, get AI-powered medicine suggestions by describing symptoms, chat with pharmacists, track orders, and receive notifications.

🏥 Pharmacists can register, get approved by admin, manage their inventory and orders, and chat with customers directly.

🛡️ Admins can approve pharmacists, manage users, view payments, and monitor platform statistics.
